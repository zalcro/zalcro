# Zalcro

**Plan before you build.**

Zalcro sits between your idea and your coding tool. Describe what you want to build, and Zalcro runs a structured planning conversation, asking the questions a tech lead would ask, then generates the documents your build actually needs to succeed.

No more bugs that turn out to be planning gaps. No more frontend that doesn't connect to the backend. No more prompts that send your coding agent in circles.

---

## What Zalcro generates

**Architecture Design Document (ADD)**
A structured document covering your system architecture, component breakdown, technology decisions, dependencies, and implementation risks. The foundation every serious build needs.

**System Diagram**
A visual map of your system — components, data flows, and how everything connects. Generated as a Mermaid diagram, ready to share or embed.

**Prompt Pack**
Paste-ready prompts built for your platform. Each prompt is scoped, sequenced, and structured so your vibe coding tool builds in the right direction from the start.

**Implementation Plan & Tickets**
A phased build sequence broken into granular, dependency-aware tickets. Ready to paste into Linear, Jira, or hand directly to Claude Code or Cursor.

---

## Who it's for

**Vibe coders**
Building on Lovable, Bolt, or Replit? Zalcro gives you the planning foundation that makes your prompts more coherent and your builds more stable. You get a Prompt Pack — sequenced, platform-ready prompts that give your tool the architectural context it needs to build the right thing in the right order.

**Agentic builders**
Using Claude Code, Cursor, or another coding agent? Zalcro generates a complete implementation plan with granular, dependency-aware tickets your agent can execute without asking clarifying questions. The kind of structured handoff that turns a capable agent into a disciplined one.
